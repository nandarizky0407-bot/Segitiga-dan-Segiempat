Four triangles, one big idea

90°A right triangle has exactly one right angle; its two acute angles always make 90° together.
60°Every angle of an equilateral triangle is 180° ÷ 3 = 60°, whatever its size.
2 = 2Isosceles: two equal sides always face two equal base angles.
180°Scalene or not — the angles of every triangle always total 180°.
Golden ruleEqual sides ↔ equal angles. Use it to check every answer!
